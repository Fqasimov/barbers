# yazılnow — 3-minute pitch

About 420 words: three minutes at a calm pace. Slide changes are in brackets.

---

**[Slide 1 — the opening animation plays]**

Good morning. We are yazılnow. Our idea in one sentence: find a free time with Baku's barbers and salons, and book it in one tap.

**[Slide 2 — Problem]**

Think about the last time you booked a haircut. You probably wrote to the salon on Instagram or WhatsApp, and then you waited. That is normal in Baku. When we checked thirty-seven top-rated salons, about six in ten were taking bookings through DMs and phone calls. Look at this chat: "Do you have a place tomorrow at six?" "Let me check." Two hours later: "Sorry, we're full."

Salons do have tools, like DIKIDI or Rezneed, but each one shows only its own salon. Not a single app shows you free times across the whole city. And the international apps are expensive: Fresha takes twenty percent of a new client's bill.

**[Slide 3 — Solution]**

Our solution is simple: every free time in the city, on one map. Clients see real free times nearby, compare salons side by side and book in four steps, without calling. Salons pay one flat monthly price, with unlimited masters and zero commission. And reviews can only come from real visits.

**[Slide 4 — Product for clients]**

This is the real app. You open it and see the nearest free times today. You open a salon, pick a service and a master, choose a time, and you're booked. You get reminders a day and two hours before.

**[Slide 5 — Product for salons]**

For salons there is a panel that works in the app and in any browser: a calendar, masters, services, statistics and one-time QR codes. The master shows the QR after the service, and the client leaves a review before leaving the chair.

**[Slide 6 — Beta test]**

Five people tested it on our test server: three clients, a salon owner and a barber. *(Read one or two of the real tester notes here.)*

**[Slide 7 — Market]**

We start in Baku, a city of about two point three million people. Salons already have tools for themselves; clients have nothing. And price matters: a salon with five masters pays about one hundred eighty-seven manat a month on Booksy. With us, forty-nine.

**[Slide 8 — Business model]**

Three plans: Sadə for ten manat, a place on the map with QR reviews. Start for twenty-nine, full online booking. And Pro for forty-nine, with branches, deposits, a waitlist and statistics. The first thirty days are free, no card needed.

**[Slide 9 — Where we are]**

This is not a mock-up. The app runs on iPhone, Android and the web, in Azerbaijani, Russian and English, with a server, an admin panel and a website. Our next steps: connect payments, go live on yazilnow.az, and sign our first founding salons.
